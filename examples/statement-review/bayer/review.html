Why this page exists. Everything this model says can be said as a plain statement, and every statement points at where it came from: a box on a drawing sheet, a page of a report, a field of a model. Checking a sample of statements against their sources turns “does this look right?” into a measured error rate, and lets someone who knows the unit check it in plain language without reading JSON.

Two separate verdicts, never mixed. Matches its source: does the drawing box or report page say this? Anyone can check that. True in the plant: is it actually so on the unit today? Only an operator, a walkdown or live data can say, and only for a statement that matched its source (the second box stays locked until the first says match). The “machine” line, where shown, only proves the export says what the model says, not that the model is right.
